Python plot shows the Desired Force vs. Displacement curve for a center detent.
Equation used was F_desired(x) = -x exp(-(x/w)^2), where w = 0.30 and controls the width of the detent.
Plot was normalized to Displacement [-1,1] and Force [-1,1]

Note for Mechanical Parts: the pictures of the Mechanical Parts presents 3D printed parts of earlier iterations. Only the arm was refined for the final version (CAD shows this as the shape for the magnet holder was altered).
